Stat Card Kit

Internal asset page — not part of the public site (noindex). Ten cards, two formats each: 1080×1350 for Instagram/social, 1200×630 for link previews and OG images. To export: resize the browser viewport to at least 1080px wide, screenshot each .stat-card element at 100% zoom, crop to its exact box. Every stat is sourced — see the Mental Load Library for full citations.

Four of the 1200×630 cards below are wired as og:image for the site (see B4) and are marked OG TARGET — export those first, into images/og/ with the exact filename shown on the card.
