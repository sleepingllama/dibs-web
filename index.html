Before you ask

07 / 07
Is it free?

Free during early access. Get in now, help shape it, and keep the good karma. Pricing comes later — early crews will hear it from us first.

What if someone in my house never claims anything?

The wheel makes the week visible to everyone: open chores sit there, in everyone’s pocket, quietly asking. You never have to be the one who says it. Most crews find the open slices get claimed faster than they expect; turns out people step up when stepping up is visible.

Couples, families, or roommates?

All three. Two people or seven, kids included — if they can tap, they can call it. Every member gets their own color on the wheel.

Does making chores visible actually help?

Yes, and it’s not just a nice idea. Research on couples finds that agreeing on who’s doing what predicts relationship satisfaction better than who’s actually doing more of it.4 Seeing the same week matters more than getting the math exactly right. That’s the whole bet behind the wheel.

Is this just for couples?

No. The invisible-remembering research isn’t limited to couples. The same recurring-vs-one-off structure shows up wherever people share a household and responsibilities.8 The wheel doesn’t care who’s dating who; it just needs everyone’s chores in one place.

Does it sync with our calendars?

Yes. Claimed chores land on your calendar automatically, so “Thursday: trash” lives next to “Thursday: standup.” Your week, one place.

Android?

iPhone first. Android is on the list, and yes, someone already called dibs on building it.
