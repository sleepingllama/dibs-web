Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Overview

Dibs is built for households. We collect only what is necessary to make the app work, and we never sell your data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following when you use Dibs:

  • Account info: email address and display name used to create your account
  • Facebook Login info: your Facebook app-scoped user ID, name, email address, and profile picture, when available and authorized by you
  • Household data: chores, assignments, schedules, and message board posts shared within your household
  • Device info: push notification tokens to deliver reminders to your device
  • Usage data: crash reports and basic analytics to improve app stability

We collect this information directly from you, automatically from your use of Dibs, or from Facebook when you choose to use Facebook Login. Dibs only receives Facebook information that you authorize Facebook to share with us.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate and sync your household data across devices
  • To authenticate you and create or link your Dibs account when you use Facebook Login
  • To send push notifications and daily summary reminders you configure
  • To diagnose crashes and fix bugs
  • To respond to support requests

3. Facebook and Meta Platform Data

If you choose Facebook Login, Dibs processes the Facebook information described above only to authenticate you and provide your Dibs account. We do not sell Meta Platform Data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties except service providers that help us operate Dibs or when required by law.

You can revoke Dibs' access to your Facebook account at any time from Facebook's Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites. Revoking access prevents Dibs from receiving additional Facebook data, but it does not automatically delete information already stored by Dibs. To delete that information, follow the instructions on our Data Deletion page.

4. Data Storage and Retention

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Firestore and Authentication), which is hosted on Google Cloud infrastructure. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

We retain personal data while your account is active and as needed to provide Dibs. After a verified deletion request, we delete or de-identify personal data associated with your account within 30 days, except information we must retain to comply with law, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, or protect Dibs and its users. Shared household records may remain in de-identified form so other household members can retain an accurate history.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. We share data only in the following circumstances:

  • With Firebase/Google as our infrastructure provider
  • With Crashlytics for crash reporting
  • When required by law or to protect our rights

6. Household Members

When you join or create a household, your display name and chore activity are visible to other members of that household. Leaving a household removes your active membership, but historical chore records may remain for continuity. If you delete your account, retained household history is de-identified as described above.

7. Your Choices and Rights

You may ask to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or revoke your consent where applicable, by contacting support@dibs.app. These choices are available to all Dibs users. See our Data Deletion page for deletion instructions. Verified deletion requests are completed within 30 days, subject to the limited retention described above.

8. Children

Dibs is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. We'll notify you of significant changes via the app. Continued use after changes are posted constitutes acceptance.

10. Contact

Dibs is responsible for the personal information described in this policy. For privacy questions, access or correction requests, and data deletion requests, contact support@dibs.app.
